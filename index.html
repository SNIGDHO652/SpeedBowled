SpeedBowled: Cricket Ball Speed Detector

Instructions

  • 1. Capture the video such that the ball is distinguishable and visible throughout the video.
  • 2. Select the approximate color of the ball for better accuracy.
  • 3. Use high frame rate and high-resolution video for better detection and accuracy.
  • 4. The average speed displayed is the most accurate for the entire video!
  • 5. Record the ball being bowled through air, not through any liquid or fluid.
  • 6. Keep the camera static. Make the video as short as possible.

Advantages

  • 1. Needs only 1 camera video input; no need for camera focal length or ball size.
  • 2. Considers acceleration due to gravity and air resistance. Works from any camera angle.
  • 3. No need to manually select the release or impact time of the ball.
  • 4. Cost-effective with no heavy machinery or technology required.
  • 5. Tracks instantaneous speed with respect to the ball's air trajectory.
